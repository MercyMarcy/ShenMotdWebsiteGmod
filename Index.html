Shenanigans Inc.

Welcome to our Garry's Mod server! Please read and follow the rules below.

Server Rules

1. Be Respectful
No harassment, discrimination, hate speech, slurs, or excessive toxicity.
2. No Cheating or Exploiting
Hacks, exploits, and abusing unintended game mechanics are prohibited.
3. No Prop Abuse
No prop killing, prop spamming, prop blocking, or intentionally causing server lag.
4. Respect Other Players
Do not grief, destroy builds, or interfere with others without permission.
5. No Mic or Chat Spam
Avoid micspamming, excessive soundboards, and repeated or disruptive messages.
6. Keep Content Appropriate
No NSFW content, offensive sprays, or sexually explicit conversations.
7. No Advertising
Do not advertise other servers, communities, or unrelated services.
8. Follow Staff Instructions
Cooperate with staff and respect their decisions. Report issues through our Discord.
9. No Server Disruption
Do not intentionally crash, lag, or otherwise disrupt the server.

Additional Information

Staff may take action against disruptive behavior not specifically covered by these rules.

Enjoy your time on Shenanigans Inc.!
